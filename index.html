Toxo Genotyper

Genotype Toxoplasma gondii from Sanger reads of the standard typing markers. The tool assembles forward and reverse reads and calls the allele at each marker. It also cuts each sequence in silico to give the PCR-RFLP type, and matches the multilocus profile to a ToxoDB genotype number. Positions that need an expert eye are flagged, with the chromatogram one click away. Your files are read in this browser and are never uploaded.

Electronic PCR-RFLP

Each consensus is cut in silico with the enzymes of the multilocus PCR-RFLP scheme (Su et al. 2006, 2010), on the nested PCR product. The pattern is named by the reference strains that share it: I = GT1, II = ME49, III = VEG.

"I or II": the enzymes at this locus do not separate those types. "u-1", "u-2": non-archetypal patterns, named as in Su et al. when a panel strain carries them. "new": matches no reference strain. "ND": part of the PCR product was not sequenced and the references differ there. Select a call to see its virtual gel. ToxoDB genotype: the genotype numbers (#1, #2, …) in the ToxoDB PCR-RFLP reference table whose profile fits every locus typed here; with fewer loci typed, several genotypes can fit.

Files and pairing

Sample names come from the file names. If two reads from the same sample were not paired, give them the same sample name here and choose Re-run.

Reference panel

Calls are only as good as this panel. References can be amplicons or whole genes. When PCR primers are known for a marker, samples are compared over the region between them; otherwise over the region every reference covers. To use your own panel, load a FASTA file whose headers read >MARKER|Allele name|optional note, for example >SAG3|Type II|GenBank AB123456. Add primers as a header line with no sequence: >SAG3|primers|FORWARD|REVERSE.

Settings
Mott's algorithm on .ab1 quality scores. 0.05 matches the Geneious default.
Use full length when your sequences span the whole gene (for example nanopore consensus sequences) and you want every difference counted.
For markers with known inner primers (SAG1, alt.SAG2), compare between the inner primers instead of the outer ones.
Second-highest peak as a fraction of the highest. Above this, the base is written as an ambiguity code.
Method: reads are matched to a marker and oriented by shared 12-mers, trimmed with Mott's algorithm (when quality scores exist), assembled by overlap alignment, then aligned to every reference. A call needs zero differences across the comparison region, which is the stretch between the PCR primers when they are known. Forward/reverse disagreements are excluded and reported. Ambiguity codes at positions that distinguish references raise a mixed-infection flag. Proof of concept for the VEuPathDB / ToxoDB community. The reference panel is a sample and is not yet complete.
